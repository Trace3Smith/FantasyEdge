📊 Player Rankings

Rankings for every supported sport, driven by the same valuation model the rest of the app uses.

  • 📈
    Multi-sport rankings Free
    MLB, NBA, WNBA, NHL, and NFL rankings — roto category value for the category sports, projection-based value for NFL.
  • 🔥
    HOT / COLD form badges — with the reason Free
    A hot or cold badge tells you why, not just that — the exact stats driving it (e.g. ".367 AVG, 6 HR over the last 15"). For MLB, NHL, NBA, and WNBA, badges come from fixed performance thresholds in each category, calculated for every player on an active roster — not just the top names — and appear during each sport's season. (NFL works a little differently — see below.)
  • 🏈
    NFL HOT / COLD — scored to your league Free
    NFL is a points game, so form is measured in fantasy points per game over the last few weeks, with a separate bar for QBs, RBs, WRs, and TEs. Every signed-in account gets a badge on standard PPR. Link your ESPN league (Premium) and the badges recompute under your league's actual scoring — your PPR type, TD values, and bonuses — with the "elite" bar scaled to how your league scores, so a high-scoring league doesn't just make everyone look hot. "Hot" then means hot in your league.
  • ⭐
    Star ratings = category coverage Free
    For the category sports (MLB, NHL, NBA, WNBA), a player's stars reflect how many roto categories they meaningfully contribute to — five stars is a true all-around contributor, one star a single-category specialist. NFL is a points league, so it swaps stars for a consistency score instead (below).
  • 📶
    NFL consistency score + ceiling Free
    A points league cares about weekly reliability, so each NFL skill player gets a consistency score — how close a bad week is to a typical week (higher = steadier, so a boom-bust flex reads low and a target hog reads high). Its ceiling (a big week's upside) sits right beside it, kept separate so a high ceiling never counts against a player's consistency.
  • 🗓️
    Season / last-15 / last-30 windows + sortable columns Free
    Switch any board between full-season, last-15, and last-30-game views, and click any stat column to re-sort by it. A Games Played column shows the games a player actually appeared in versus how many their team has played (a straight appearance count for pitchers and NHL goalies, who don't play every game).
  • 🧮
    Combine categories Free
    Check two or more stat columns and the board instantly re-ranks by combined strength in just those categories — e.g. find the players who help most in AVG + SB at once. Ideal for targeting a specific roster weakness on the waiver wire.
  • 🎯
    Tap a hot player, jump to their stats Free
    Click any hot player anywhere in the app and you land straight on their full stat card in Rankings — no searching for them again.
  • 🏈
    NFL value model — projections + prior year Free
    NFL rankings are built from real projections and prior-year performance, not just raw current-season stats. Once the season starts, they also factor in team-context signals like target share and pace — in the offseason those are shown as context only and don't move the ranking yet.
  • ⛳
    PGA rankings Premium
    Golf rankings are Premium; all other sports' rankings are free.

🎛️ Team Manager Premium

Link your ESPN account once (cookies stored server-side, same login covers every sport) and manage your lineups. The entire Team Manager is Premium.

  • 🤖
    Lineup Autopilot — MLB & WNBA Premium
    Sets your optimal legal lineup automatically each morning, valuing players under your league's own detected scoring (MLB roto z-score, WNBA H2H Points). One-tap "apply optimal lineup" too.
  • 🩹
    IL/IR management with smart swaps Premium
    Detects your league's real IL/IR slot, activates recovered players, and moves injured starters to the IL — pairing swaps so nothing overfills your roster.
  • 📥
    Waiver suggestions — standings-weighted category impact Premium
    For MLB roto, only surfaces a waiver add if it wins more categories than it loses, weighting each category by where you actually rank — gaining a category you're last in matters far more than padding one you already lead.
  • ✂️
    Drop recommendations Premium
    Names the specific weakest droppable player (by value under your scoring) when a roster move is needed — display-only, since drops are irreversible.
  • 🔔
    Prospect call-up system Premium
    Tracks stashed minor-league prospects and alerts you the moment one is called up. Flag any prospect with Watch to get a one-tap Reclaim on ESPN prompt if they're promoted, and drop suggestions carry days-in-minors context (60+ days flags a long stash).

🤝 Trade Center Premium

Reads every team's roster in your linked leagues and reasons about trades with our player values. The entire Trade Center is Premium.

  • 🔎
    Trade Scanner Premium
    Finds 2–3 win-win trades across your league — who to target, what you give, what you get, and a fairness score.
  • ⚖️
    Trade Evaluator Premium
    Paste or describe any incoming offer and get a call — accept, reject, or counter — grounded in both rosters and our values.
  • 🔁
    Counter Generator Premium
    Asks for 2–3 ranked counter-offers that swing the deal in your favor while staying realistic enough to get accepted.
  • 🚪
    Walk Away Detector Premium
    Calls out when you're being lowballed so you know when the right move is simply to pass.
  • 🎯
    Category-aware valuation Premium
    Values trades by how they move your category standings — not just raw player value — including outlier warnings on players whose recent line is out of step with their track record.

📋 Draft Mode

One draft board, two ways to run it — plus a free mock draft against AI opponents. The AI Coach advises you on every pick.

  • 📝
    Offline draft board Premium
    Track a real league draft (Zoom or in person) — mark each pick as it happens and the board, roster, and Coach update live.
  • 🔗
    Linked draft — Sleeper Premium
    Paste your Sleeper draft link and we auto-track every pick live (no Sleeper login needed), with live value suggestions on your turn and a post-pick grade for every pick — value over the next-best available at that slot.
  • 🎯
    Mock Draft Free
    Draft against AI opponents that pick at realistic value. Tap any player to open a detail sidebar with their category-by-category stats and prior-year numbers, right on the draft board. Free accounts get 1 mock per day with AI Coach advice through round 7; Premium unlocks unlimited mocks and advice every round.
  • 💬
    AI Draft Coach Premium
    A conversational coach embedded in Draft Mode and the Trade Center that already knows your roster and settings. Free mock users see the analyst's pick through round 7; follow-up questions are Premium.

🏆 Brackets & Bowls

Data-backed game picks. NFL Pick'em is live now; the weekly college football, bowl, and March Madness sections light up in their seasons.

  • 🏈
    NFL Pick'em Free
    Every game's pick and market-implied win % — read straight from the live betting market — plus key injuries and game-week weather. Highest-confidence picks and upset alerts are flagged. Informational — not betting advice.
  • 🏈
    College Football — weekly Top-25 Free
    A week-by-week view of the Top-25 matchups, each with its market-implied win %, injuries, and weather — the same treatment as NFL Pick'em, on the college slate. Seasonal — fills in through the college football season.
  • 🏈
    College Bowl & Playoff picks Free
    Same win-probability picks, injuries, and weather for every bowl and College Football Playoff game. Seasonal — matchups are set in December, so the tab fills in as bowls are announced.
  • 🏀
    March Madness bracket optimizer Premium
    Every men's tournament game's pick and win probability — current-year team strength (ESPN BPI) blended with historical seed performance — plus round-by-round advancement odds, national title probabilities, and a recommended optimal bracket. Upset alerts flag live first-round underdogs. Seasonal — the field is set on Selection Sunday in March. (Pool-strategy leverage from national pick popularity isn't included — that data has no reliable free source, and we don't fake it.)
