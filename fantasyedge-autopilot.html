How do I find these cookies?
  1. Log in to fantasy.espn.com in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox on a computer.
  2. Open DevTools (F12 or right-click → Inspect) and go to the Application tab (Chrome/Edge) or Storage tab (Firefox).
  3. Under Cookies, click https://fantasy.espn.com.
  4. Find the row named espn_s2 and copy its full Value into the first box above.
  5. Find the row named SWID and copy its Value (including the curly braces) into the second box.
  6. Click Connect ESPN. We'll verify the cookies with ESPN before saving them.
